### 2026-10-27 — KAR-471: crashed cars and knocked-down citizens stay on the road

- **Defect:** `car3/AfterCrashBehavior` servoed a crashed car toward the terrain + 3 m and clipped it up to that height; `citizen/RecoverBehavior` clipped a knocked-down citizen up to it. In a road cutting (terrain above the road) that threw them onto the hillside; on a deck the car was pressed down against the deck's collider.
- **Fix:** the player's downward probe moved into `engine.physics.HoverSurfaceProbe` (`SurfaceBelow`, `IsProbeSurface`), with `VehicleTargetHeight` (the built surface + clearance where there is one, the terrain only over open ground — so NPC cars, crashed or driving, ride the same height, KAR-470) and `NeedsRescue` (only far below the terrain, falling, and with nothing built underneath). The crashed car aims at `VehicleTargetHeight` and is rescued instead of clipped; the citizen is clipped to the terrain only over open ground. The player's own target is unchanged and now uses the shared probe.
- Flat city: a crashed car aims at the same height as before (floor plane + clearance = terrain + 3 m); a walking citizen's body is at ground + 3.05 m, above the old clip, so nothing a player saw there changes.
- Tests: `HoverSurfaceProbeTests` (vehicle target in a cutting and over nothing, flat-city equality, rescue cases, probe surface rule; source scans pin all three callers to the shared probe).
- Found on the way: the player's own rule, `max(terrain + 3 m, surface + 1 m)`, floats the ship above the road wherever the terrain beside it is higher — at 43.8 % of ground-street points in eight terrain cities, median 0.55 m, > 1 m at 10.9 %, worst 10.4 m. Left for the owner to decide.
