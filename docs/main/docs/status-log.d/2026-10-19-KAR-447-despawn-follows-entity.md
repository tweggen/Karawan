- ✅ **TALE S0 W3: despawn follows the entity; the player's NPCs are kept explicitly (KAR-447, 2026-10-19)**: `TaleSpawnOperator.PurgeFragment` used to clear the materialised flag for NPCs whose *schedule position* lay in the purged fragment, but an entity dies with the fragment that *created* it; NPCs walking across a 400 m border could therefore never respawn, or get a second entity. Now an ownership map (`engine.tale.TaleEntityOwnership`, npcId → owner fragment + entity, written in the entity-setup action with `InCreation--`) decides: purging a fragment dematerialises exactly the NPCs it owned and records where each entity stood (`TaleManager.OnNpcDematerialized`, which never removes a schedule); each poll dematerialises NPCs whose entity the wiper took while the fragment stayed loaded; the spawn status reports live bucketed NPCs as resident (`SpawnStatus.Dead`), so spawns are queued only for missing NPCs instead of `MinCharacters` no-op spawns per fragment per poll; a spawn whose fragment was purged during creation drops its entity. `NpcSchedule.IsKnownToPlayer` (flag, `Trust[-1]` or `player_fact.*`) and `TaleManager.MarkPlayerContact` (sets the flag and the skip mask; used by `TaleModule`'s two writers) make "the player's NPCs are never forgotten" explicit; an unflagged known schedule is warned about and flagged. Deleted: `ForgetSchedule` and the Tier-2 branch of `TerminateCharacters` (dead code: TALE NPCs are never despawned by distance), which is now empty; Tier 2 is documented unused. The ImGui "Flush" button now releases fragments on the logical thread. Tests: `DespawnBookkeepingTests` (10). Docs: TALE ARCHITECTURE §6.3, REFERENCE, PHASE_6.
