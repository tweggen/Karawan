### 2026-10-25 — KAR-465: no edge where a road leaves a junction (stroke colliders follow the drawn carriageway)

- **Report:** driving up a bridge ramp, the car collides with the "bridge top" and cannot continue.
- **Measured, not assumed:** the ramp-meets-deck junction is not it — all 1 474 deck junctions in the shipped world have two arms (no cap slab), are straight within 0.21° and their colliders met exactly. The edge is the junction cap slab (flat at the junction's height) standing out of every branch that falls away from a junction: each stroke's collider was a box from junction centre to junction centre, while the mesh holds each end flat at the junction's height. 32 484 street ends over 0.5 m, 2 812 over 1 m, 48 ramp feet over 1 m, worst 2.3 m; on rising branches the same box was an invisible ridge across the junction.
- **Fix:** `engine.streets.generation.CarriagewayCollider` — convex prisms over the four corners the mesh is built on, at the junction heights, cut into axial slices where oblique ends twist the road (slice length × kerb slope difference ≤ 0.2 m); overlapping ends get one prism. Every branch now meets its slab within 0.1 mm; the collider lies within 4.7 cm of the drawn road at the 99th percentile, 0.17 m at worst, never below it; 5.0 prisms per stroke instead of one box. `DeckCollider` keeps only `IsNeededFor`; stroke and cap hulls share one emission helper. No `DbVersion` bump (colliders are built at fragment load).
- Tests: `CarriagewayColliderTests` (shapes, slicing, and the seam and surface over eight terrain cities with grade separation). Removed the box tests in `DeckColliderTests` and the two `JunctionColliderTests` that pinned the old box behaviour. In-game check pending: drive up a ramp and into junctions on hills without catching an edge.
